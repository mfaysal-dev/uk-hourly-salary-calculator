How to convert hourly pay to a salary

To turn an hourly rate into a yearly salary, multiply it by the hours you’re paid each week and then by the number of paid weeks in a year (52 if your holiday is paid). To go the other way, divide your salary by your paid weeks and then by your weekly hours.

  • Hourly → yearly: hourly rate × hours a week × 52
  • Yearly → monthly: salary ÷ 12
  • Yearly → hourly: salary ÷ 52 ÷ hours a week

For example, £15 an hour for 37.5 hours a week is £562.50 a week, £2,437.50 a month and £29,250 a year. A £30,000 salary at 37.5 hours a week works out at £15.38 an hour.

The National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage from April 2026

From 1 April 2026 the National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over is £12.71 an hour (up from £12.21). The National Minimum Wage is £10.85 for 18 to 20-year-olds, and £8.00 for under-18s and apprentices. Almost all workers are entitled to it, including part-time, casual, agency and zero-hours workers. Self-employed people and company directors aren’t.

A full-time worker on the National Living Wage doing 37.5 hours a week earns £24,784.50 a year before tax. See what that leaves after tax and National Insurance with the take-home pay calculator.

What counts as working time

Your average hourly pay for minimum wage purposes is worked out over each pay reference period. Some things can reduce it, such as unpaid overtime, unpaid training, travel between jobs and charges for uniform. Accommodation provided by your employer can count towards the minimum wage up to a daily limit. If any of this applies, use GOV.UK’s official checker.

Hourly pay and minimum wage FAQs

What is the minimum wage in 2026?

From 1 April 2026 the National Living Wage is £12.71 an hour for workers aged 21 and over. The National Minimum Wage is £10.85 for 18 to 20-year-olds and £8.00 for under-18s and apprentices.

How do I convert an hourly rate to an annual salary?

Multiply your hourly rate by your paid hours per week, then by 52. For example, £15 an hour × 37.5 hours × 52 weeks = £29,250 a year.

How much is £30,000 a year per hour?

At 37.5 hours a week, £30,000 a year is about £15.38 an hour (£576.92 a week). At 40 hours a week it’s about £14.42 an hour.

How much is a full-time salary on the National Living Wage?

At £12.71 an hour, 37.5 hours a week for 52 weeks comes to £24,784.50 a year. At 40 hours a week it’s £26,436.80.

Who gets the apprentice rate?

Apprentices aged under 19, and apprentices aged 19 or over who are in the first year of their apprenticeship. After the first year, apprentices aged 19 or over get the minimum wage for their age.

When does the minimum wage go up?

The rates change on 1 April every year. This calculator uses the rates for the date you choose, back to April 2024.

What can I do if I’m paid less than the minimum wage?

Talk to your employer first and check your payslips. You can contact Acas for free advice or complain to HMRC, which can make your employer pay back what you’re owed.
